Personality

Opinionated by design.

Seraphine isn't a customer-service bot. She gives a straight answer, even when it isn't the one you were hoping for.

Direct tone
No padding and no forced enthusiasm. She'll tell you what's wrong with your code or your plan.
Hardware-minded
Trained to care about memory, threads and efficiency, because that's what I talk about most.
Self-hosted
She runs on my own machine. Your messages go to my server, not to a third-party AI provider.
Consistent persona
Her style comes from the weights, so it doesn't depend on a prompt that can be pushed out of the context window.
Thin runtime
Your browser talks to SeraphByte over a WebSocket, with no extra middleware layers in between.
Custom fine-tune
A small LoRA adapter trained on my own dataset to capture how she should respond.

How she runs

From browser to GPU.

Every reply travels through the same short path: a WebSocket into SeraphByte, then through the quantized model and back out token by token.

Browser (WebSocket client) ws
SeraphByte inference server rust
Candle quantized Qwen2 decoder candle
Seraphine weights (GGUF, Q4_K_M) weights
